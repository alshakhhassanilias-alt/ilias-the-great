## Gameplay (authentic, captured from the game)
All map, interface, WAR, war room, VICTORY, negotiation and blocs shots are real-time captures of this game build
(seed 7, playing Venezuela), recorded frame by frame with `capture-gameplay.mjs`. Nothing in them was painted,
composited or altered beyond a light, uniform grade (contrast/vignette/grain). About 26 s of the 75.6 s (~35%).
