♞ Ilias the Great

Camera chess coach for over-the-board games. Point it at a real board and it shows the best move on the live feed. Everyone at the table should know it's on.

Start the camera to begin.
  1. Start the camera and look down on the board from a side or from above. Keep it steady (phone on a stack of books works).
  2. Click the four corners of the 8×8 playing area in this order: a1 (White's left-hand corner), h1, h8, a8. Drag the dots to fine-tune. Use Rotate 90° if the labels sit on the wrong corners.
  3. Set up the pieces, then just play. Each time a move is made and your hand leaves the board, the app works out what moved and shows the engine's best reply as an arrow.
