Allow the camera to begin.
  1. Open this page on your phone and allow the camera. Point it at the board so all 64 squares are in view. Propping the phone up works best, but holding it is OK.
  2. It finds the board by itself (the grid appears), then asks which side you're playing. Tap it, or just say “white” or “black”.
  3. Play. When it's your turn, an arrow on the camera shows the best move (and it says it out loud). It follows every move by watching the board.
  4. If it can't find the board, use Set corners by hand and tap a1 (White's left-hand corner), h1, h8, a8.
